LEGAL

Nature of the service.

Vidimus is an electronic anchoring service. It records the integrity, the time and the origin of business records cryptographically, with electronic signatures, a hash-chained ledger and external timestamps, and delivers the proof of this to the customer in a bundle that can be verified independently. For the content of the records, Vidimus is a processor on behalf of the customer; for the mandate, the agreements and the accounts, Vidimus is itself the controller.

Vidimus itself is not a qualified trust service provider within the meaning of eIDAS. What is included is a qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST, a qualified trust service provider on the EU trusted list: usually within six hours, the batch root of records from customer organisations comes under such a timestamp, via an aggregate root, as long as the annual package with that provider has room. For that timestamp, the presumption applies of the accuracy of the date and time it indicates and of the integrity of the data to which it is bound (eIDAS Art. 41(2)). The record is linked to it through hash calculations you can recompute yourself (Merkle path, Evidence Record). Whether the presumption also applies to the record itself is for the court to assess. For the rest: what is anchored cannot be refused solely because it is electronic, and its evidential value is weighed freely (Art. 8.11 of the Belgian Civil Code). Parties can agree that these records count as evidence between them, without prejudice to the right to prove the contrary (Art. 8.2 of the Belgian Civil Code). Vidimus supplies evidence, not legal advice.

PRIVACY

Short, because it can be short.

  • No cookies. This website uses no cookies and contains no trackers or third-party analytics scripts.
  • The calculation module processes everything in your browser. The figures you enter are not stored anywhere and do not leave your device.
  • Booking a demo goes through Cal.com (separate window). Cal.com processes your name, email address and chosen time to set up the appointment and send you a confirmation. Nothing is loaded or tracked on this website itself in the process.
  • If you email us, we process your name, email address and the content of your message in order to reply (legal basis: your request, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep that correspondence for as long as the conversation runs and delete it no later than 24 months after the last contact.
  • Prospecting: if we contact you without a prior relationship, we do so on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), stating the source of your data. A single reply with "stop" is enough to have all your data deleted.
  • Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection — email oskar@vo-initiatives.com. Complaints can be lodged with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
  • In the product itself only cryptographic hashes go to Bitcoin, never the data itself. Those hashes are pseudonymised: once the content and the salt are deleted, they can no longer be linked to a person. The underlying data stays outside Bitcoin and can be deleted.
