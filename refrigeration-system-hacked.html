VIDIMUS/QUESTIONS/REFRIGERATION SYSTEM HACKED

Your refrigeration system has been hacked: what can you still show afterwards?

Short answerWhat is stored only in the attacked system can be called into question. If you measure with a separate logger and also record every measurement outside your systems straight away, you can show afterwards when the temperature rose and for how long. Vidimus does not stop an attack. It makes sure an insurer or customer can check that series themselves.

Updated on 3 November 2026 · Oskar Verschoren, Vidimus (VO Initiatives BV, Antwerp)

01 / WHAT HAPPENED

What happened.

  • The attack. During the night of 1 to 2 November 2026, evaporators of refrigeration systems in Belgian shops were remotely switched to defrost mode, so that fridges and freezers warmed up. That is what a refrigeration installer told VRT NWS.
  • The scale. Dozens of shops from different chains, and also butchers. In the Netherlands, at least 17 businesses are affected, mainly local shops and butchers (VRT NWS, citing NOS). A complete overview was not available on 2 November.
  • The damage. One shop operator estimates the damage at a minimum of €60,000 in food thrown away (VRT NWS).
  • The investigation. The federal prosecutor's office opened a criminal investigation. Who is behind it was not known on 3 November.
  • Wider than Belgium. In October, Forescout counted 12,976 exposed management services of refrigeration systems on the internet, in 99 countries. These are exposed systems, not hacked ones (Euronews).
02 / FASFC

What the FASFC asks.

  • Measure it yourself. According to the FASFC (the Belgian food safety agency), all shops fall under self-checking: they test themselves whether products became too warm, by measuring the refrigeration and certainly also the products (VRT NWS).
  • The limits, as reported. Chilled usually 7 °C, sensitive products such as meat and fish sometimes 4 °C, frozen -18 °C core temperature (VRT NWS). Check the limit for your product with the FASFC.
  • Exceeded or in doubt. Then the product comes off the shelves and may no longer be sold.
  • The highest temperature counts. A Ghent University researcher, speaking to VRT NWS, points to a risk analysis and to the maximum temperature the product reached. According to that researcher, the law does not currently allow that as a way out.
03 / SECURE

Secure it first.

  • Take the management off the open internet. An ethical hacker, speaking to VRT NWS, recommends making such systems reachable only through the internal network, for example with a VPN. A refrigeration installer advised securing the connection and, in the worst case, disconnecting it.
  • Individual logins instead of shared passwords, with a second factor, and replacing devices that no longer receive security updates (Forescout, via Euronews).
  • Ask your installer who can access your system remotely, and whether the updates have been installed.
  • This is the work of your installer and your IT partner. Vidimus does not do this.
04 / AFTERWARDS

What you want to be able to show afterwards.

  1. A separate loggerseparate from the system

    A logger with its own connection keeps measuring if the controls are compromised. A logger you already have may be enough, if it can send its measurements on.

  2. Every measurement elsewhere too, straight awayfingerprint · within five minutes

    Every measurement gets its own fingerprint and a signature, and an external timestamp within five minutes. A value changed afterwards gives a different fingerprint.

  3. A gap stays visibleno measurements = visible

    If the logger goes silent, the series shows that period as a gap. That too is information for whoever later wants to know what happened.

  4. A third party checks it themselveswithout an account

    An insurer, auditor or customer recomputes the series themselves, without an account and without having to take your word or ours for it. Vidimus does not judge, it compares.

05 / LIMITS

What Vidimus does not do.

  • Stop an attack. Vidimus is not a security product and does not see an intruder.
  • Make a measurement from the attacked system itself reliable. What is anchored is what that system passed on.
  • Record measurements from before the connection. What has already happened cannot be added afterwards.
  • Show that the logger measured accurately. Calibration and placement remain your responsibility.
  • Decide what must happen to the food, or who pays. That is for you, the FASFC, your insurer or the court to assess. The reports we read do not say who bears the costs.
06 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Can Vidimus secure my refrigeration system?

No. Vidimus does not stop an attack, does not detect an intruder and does not repair anything. Security is the work of your installer and your IT partner. Vidimus comes afterwards: it makes sure you can show what was measured.

My shop was hit. Can Vidimus still help now?

Not for what has already happened: measurements from before the connection can no longer be anchored. Keep what you have, such as exports, thermometer readings and photos, and consult your insurer and the FASFC. What you find today, you can still record in the app, with photos and the signature of whoever is present.

Why a separate logger?

Because a measurement from the attacked system itself is a weak answer: Vidimus anchors what comes in, accurate or not. A logger with its own connection, separate from that system, keeps measuring if the controls are compromised.

Is my system's temperature log worthless, then?

No. As of 3 November, none of the sources we read reports that temperature logs were forged. It is about the question an insurer or customer may ask: does this come from the system that was attacked, and can someone else check that it has not changed since?
