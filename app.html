01 / WHAT IT DOES

What it does.

Your driver, technician or landlord records a delivery, damage, handover or inspection in the app: photos, the location according to the device and a signature. The recipient confirms on their own phone, without an account and without an app. Vidimus anchors the whole as one record; your customer checks it later themselves.

02 / THE PROOF

Four steps, the same for every record.

  1. 01

    Fingerprint

    sha-256 · over content, photos and location

    At the moment of recording, the record gets its fingerprint. One digit or one pixel different gives a different fingerprint.

  2. 02

    Signature

    ed25519 · key on the phone of the person signing

    Driver and recipient sign the same fingerprint, each with a key created on their own device. Two parties, one content.

  3. 03

    Timestamp

    rfc 3161 · per five-minute group

    An external timestamping service signs the group the record is in, every five minutes. The time does not come from you and not from us. Then the qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST (eIDAS) follows, per group, for customer organisations, usually within six hours.

  4. 04

    Anchor

    ledger (hash chain) → opentimestamps → bitcoin

    The root of the group goes into our ledger, where each entry hashes the previous one, and via OpenTimestamps into Bitcoin. Any change or deletion shows up for anyone who checks, even if we were to try it ourselves.

Every step can be recomputed, even without Vidimus. All seven steps, from identity to deletion

05 / QUESTIONS

What you want to know.

Does the recipient need the app?

No. The recipient confirms on their own phone, without an account and without an app. Your own people use the free app on iPhone or Android, or the web app.

What counts as one record?

One recorded moment: a delivery, damage, handover or inspection. Ten photos of the same delivery, with the signature, together make one record.

Can Vidimus see my photos?

In a regular logbook, everything is stored encrypted on servers in the EU, with a separate key per organisation. Our server opens your content only when the service needs it, such as to display, search or create an evidence bundle, and every opening appears in your access log. If you choose a confidential logbook, the phone encrypts everything before sending and only you have the key. Vidimus then sees the fingerprint and a short envelope (type, reference, source and time), not the content.
