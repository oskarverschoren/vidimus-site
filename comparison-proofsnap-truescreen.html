VIDIMUS/COMPARISON

Vidimus, ProofSnap and TrueScreen. What is the difference?

All three record digital proof with a fingerprint and a timestamp, so that someone can later check that it did not change. Each is built for a different moment. We are Vidimus, so we are not neutral. That is why every claim about the others comes with the page on their own site where we checked it on 27 October 2026. Has something changed since then? Email us and we will update it.

01 / IN ONE SENTENCE

What each is built for.

PROOFSNAP

What is on your screen, from the browser

An extension for Chrome and Edge that records a web page, a session or a video as an evidence package, including behind a login. For lawyers, landlords and compliance teams. [1][2]

TRUESCREEN

Certifying digital content, on every channel

App (iOS and Android), web portal, extension, forensic browser and API to certify photos, video, audio, web pages, chats, documents, email and meetings. [5]

VIDIMUS

Business records at the source, by both parties

Deliveries, handovers, inspections, documents, measurements and web pages, recorded at the moment itself in the app or automatically from your software, sensors or database. The counterparty co-signs or checks for themselves.

03 / HONESTLY

Where they are stronger.

  • ProofSnap records pages behind a login and entire browser sessions, with WARC and HAR, and video too. If you need proof from social media, a webshop with an account or a chat in the browser, you will find more there than at Vidimus. [2]
  • TrueScreen covers more types of content (audio, video, email, meetings), is available in six languages and shows ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 badges on their site. Vidimus has no ISO certificate. [5]
  • TrueScreen shows logos of insurers and other companies on its site. Vidimus does not yet have paying customers we may name. [5]
04 / DIFFERENT

Where Vidimus is different.

  • Recording at the moment itself, at the source. Not what is on a screen afterwards, but the delivery, the handover or the measurement while it happens, from the app or your own system.
  • Both parties. The recipient, hirer or client co-signs on the spot on their own phone, without an account. Then the proof is not about your version, but about one version.
  • Continuous, not per item. Sensors and systems send automatically; each measurement and each record is anchored, without anyone pressing a button.
  • Per use, in euros. Free up to 100 records a month, no payment details needed; the qualified timestamp on the group is included.

If you want to record afterwards what you see on a screen, including behind a login, look at ProofSnap. If you want to certify all kinds of media with an app in your own language, look at TrueScreen.
