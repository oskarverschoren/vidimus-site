01 / WHAT IT DOES

What it does.

You create each measurement source once in the app, with a unit, a range and its own key. After that, the device or your measurement platform sends its measurements to the API. Each measurement gets a fingerprint and is anchored within five minutes. In a dispute, you show the series of that device.

02 / THE PROOF

Four steps, the same for every measurement.

  1. 01

    Fingerprint

    sha-256 · per measurement

    Each measurement gets its own fingerprint, over source, time, value and unit. A corrected value gives a different fingerprint.

  2. 02

    Signature

    ed25519 · key of the measurement source, kept by Vidimus

    Each measurement source has its own key and records only for itself. The key can be revoked at any time.

  3. 03

    Timestamp

    rfc 3161 · per five-minute group

    An external timestamping service signs the group the measurement is in, every five minutes. The time does not come from you and not from us. Then the qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST (eIDAS) follows, per group, for customer organisations, usually within six hours.

  4. 04

    Anchor

    ledger (hash chain) → opentimestamps → bitcoin

    The root of the group goes into our ledger, where each entry hashes the previous one, and via OpenTimestamps into Bitcoin. Any change or deletion shows up for anyone who checks, even if we were to try it ourselves.

Every step can be recomputed, even without Vidimus. All seven steps, from identity to deletion

05 / QUESTIONS

What you want to know.

What is a measurement, and what is not?

A measurement is one number, such as a temperature, weight or position, from a sensor or logger that you created with a unit and a range. You pay per device, not per measurement. Everything a system or phone sends with its own content is a record.

What if the device is offline for a while?

Later, send the measurements that the device or your platform stored in the meantime together through /measurements/batch, in ascending time order and no older than 24 hours. The time in the measurement is what the device reported; the timestamp of the group shows when the measurement was anchored.

Do I have to replace my measurement platform?

No. Your loggers and your platform stay. The platform or the device also sends each measurement to Vidimus. You first test the integration with a test key that records nothing; then the administrator creates the live key.

Start with one device: the first sensor or logger is free.

No payment details needed. A director identifies your company once with the Belgian eID; then you create a measurement source and test the integration with a test key.
