01 · YOUR CUSTOMER'S PROBLEM

Their records are in your system. The auditor looks there too — and asks whether they have changed since.

Your system shows what was recorded. It cannot show that it has stayed untouched since — an ordinary database can change afterwards, both at your customer and at their counterparty, and the history that would show it is in the same database. As long as that cannot be proven, every delivery, measurement or approval in a dispute comes down to one word against another.

A

One word against another

The recipient says 23 pallets, your customer says 26. Both exports come from a system that is itself in dispute. Whoever has the nicest PDF wins.

B

The auditor asks for proof

The FASFC (the Belgian food safety agency), insurer, statutory auditor, court: they look in the system, and then ask whether it has stayed unchanged since and exactly when it was recorded. An export or screenshot from that same system says nothing about that.

C

Your product gets the blame

“It was in the system, surely?” Every dispute your customer loses falls back on the software in which it happened — yours.

05 · WHAT YOUR CUSTOMER GETS

Not hashing. Proof.

Never say that Vidimus vouches for the content. It establishes who, what, when, and whether anything changed afterwards — and lets everyone recompute that for themselves.

WHO

Identity

Company number checked in the Belgian company register (KBO), mandate signed with the eID, a certificate per device or system in the company's name. Every signature is Ed25519; the key stays on the device.

WHEN

Qualified time

Not our clock: an external timestamp within five minutes, and GLOBALTRUST, a qualified trust service provider on the EU trusted list, puts a qualified timestamp (eIDAS) on the groups with records of customer organisations, usually within six hours. After that, the same root goes into Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps — a second witness, public and verifiable without Vidimus.

WHAT

Unchanged

SHA-256 over the data and the attachments, per five-minute group in one Merkle tree. One byte different and the fingerprint no longer matches the anchor — even if we were to try.

WITHOUT VIDIMUS

Verification

Public check page, evidence bundle with a check script, case file for the court. Every step can be repeated with open tools. Originals encrypted per document, with a key per organisation; every decryption by our server is shown in the access log, which your customer sees for itself.

07 · QUESTIONS FROM PARTNERS

What you want to know before you sign.

May I sell it under my own name?

Yes. Co-branding (“your proof layer — powered by Vidimus”) is allowed in your product and documentation. Only the verification itself stays on the Vidimus domain: a check page with only your logo would undermine the separation between who records and who shows the proof, and that separation is exactly what your customer is buying.

Who invoices the customer?

Reseller and integrator: you. You receive one monthly invoice from us, with the discount of your tier as a separate line. Referral: we invoice the customer and pay out your share each quarter; the amount is shown in your portal every month.

What if my customer wants to go direct or to another partner?

That is allowed. The customer takes their records, keys and verification links with them; you cooperate with the handover. Running referral shares continue until the end of their 24 months.

Am I a commercial agent?

No. You sell in your own name (reseller, integrator) or bring in customers for a fee that is limited in time (referral). There is no exclusivity and no goodwill or clientele indemnity. The agreement says so.

What may I say, and what may I not?

You may say: who, what, when and whether anything changed afterwards is on record and can be recomputed without Vidimus. You may not say: that Vidimus vouches for the content, that there is a “Bitcoin guarantee”, or that a court must accept the proof. The court weighs it; we make it recomputable. The explainer pack in the portal has the right wording.

Measurement or record — what counts for the price?

Measurements from a measurement source (sensor, logger, weighbridge, GPS) are included without limit in €15 per measurement source per month in which it measures, the first one free, via /measurements. You create a measurement source with a name, a unit and a range (POST /sources, or per end customer through the partner API); only its own key may send measurements. Everything with its own content or ID — delivery, photo, CMR, approval — is a record and counts per item, per tier, via /registrations: also what your system sends, and also what still comes in through the old /ingest. See the pricing.

Who reads my customers' content?

Each organisation has its own key; originals are encrypted per document (AES-256-GCM). Your partner key never reads content. If our server reads content for a case file or bundle, that is shown in the access log, which the customer sees for itself.

What if Vidimus stops?

Evidence bundles are open formats with published verification steps (SHA-256, RFC 3161, Merkle, OpenTimestamps). They remain verifiable without Vidimus. The agreement says so, and so does the verification explanation.
