How the proof works. Four gates, for every record.

You do not have to take our word for it: every step can be recomputed with ordinary software, even without Vidimus.

  1. 01 · fingerprint

    SHA-256

    A fingerprint of the content and of every attachment. Not encryption: one character different gives a different fingerprint.

    64 characters · per record · immediately
  2. 02 · signature

    Ed25519

    Signed with the key of the device or of your system. If the recipient co-signs, they do so with their own key on their own phone.

    key per device · per system
  3. 03 · timestamp

    RFC 3161 + eIDAS

    Every five minutes one Merkle root, timestamped by Sectigo. For customer organisations, also qualified per group by GLOBALTRUST, usually within six hours.

    within 5 min · eIDAS per group
  4. 04 · anchor

    Bitcoin

    Every root goes to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps and is in a block within a few hours: a public second witness outside Vidimus.

    opentimestamps · .ots in the bundle

With a different fingerprint, the Merkle path no longer leads to the root. So the content is not in the anchored group.

The ledger only knows the original root. This content appears nowhere in the chain.

What you want to know.

The objections we hear most often, answered briefly and honestly.

All questions
Does a court accept this?

The court weighs all evidence freely. Your record is connected to the root of its group through hash computations you can recompute yourself (Merkle path, Evidence Record). If that group contains records of a customer organisation, that root carries a qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST. For that timestamp, the presumption that date and time are accurate applies throughout the EU (eIDAS, Art. 41(2)); the record therefore existed no later than that time. Whether the presumption also applies to this record is for the court to assess. Vidimus shows what was recorded; who is right is for the court to decide.

Is a fingerprint the same as encryption?

No. SHA-256 turns the content into a fingerprint of 64 characters that belongs to exactly that content: whoever has the content can recompute it, and one character different gives a different fingerprint. Encryption is something else: your documents and photos are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a separate key per document.

What if Vidimus stops?

If Vidimus stops voluntarily, we announce it at least ninety days in advance and you can take everything with you during that period; in a bankruptcy, the bankruptcy trustee decides. Your bundles remain verifiable: you can check every evidence bundle without Vidimus, with a small script or with the European Commission's software.

Can Vidimus read my data?

In a regular logbook, everything is stored encrypted on servers in the EU, with a separate key per organisation. Our server opens your content only when the service needs it, such as to display, search or create an evidence bundle, and every opening appears in your access log. Do you want Vidimus to be technically unable to read anything as well? Choose a confidential logbook: the phone or your system encrypts everything before sending and only you have the key. Vidimus then sees the fingerprint and a short envelope (type, reference, source and time), not the content. You do not need to install anything; you keep one recovery file.

Do I have to replace my software?

No. Your TMS, WMS or ERP remains the source. Vidimus records and anchors the records that matter, through the app or an integration with the API. That integration is built by your vendor or your own IT, with a separate key per system.

What counts as one record?

One recorded moment: a delivery, photo, inspection or document. Ten photos of the same delivery, with the signature, together make one record. Measurements from a sensor do not count separately.
