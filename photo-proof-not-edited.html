VIDIMUS/QUESTIONS/PHOTOS AS PROOF

How do you prove that a photo was not edited?

Short answerBy recording the photo at the moment itself: a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the file, a signature from the device and a timestamp from an external service. Whoever recomputes the photo later sees whether a single pixel changed. It does not show that the scene was real, but it does show that this file existed at that time and has remained unchanged since.

01 / HOW IT WORKS

How it works.

  1. Take the photo in the appiPhone · Android · web app

    Your team member takes the photos in the Vidimus App, at the location itself. The record contains the photos, the location according to the device and the device's time. For a delivery or handover, the counterparty confirms on their own phone, without an account and without an app.

  2. Fingerprintsha-256

    At the moment of recording, the record gets one fingerprint over the content, the photos and the location. One pixel different gives a different fingerprint. The photo cannot be reconstructed from the fingerprint.

  3. Signatureed25519 · key on the device

    The person recording signs the fingerprint with a key that was created on their own phone and never leaves the device. If the counterparty co-signs, they sign the same fingerprint with their own key.

  4. Timestamp and anchorrfc 3161 · opentimestamps

    Within five minutes, an external timestamping service signs the group the record is in; the time does not come from you and not from us. Then the root of the group goes into Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps. If the group contains records of customer organisations, the group then also receives a qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST (eIDAS), usually within six hours.

  5. Verify it yourselfcheck page · evidence bundle

    Your customer, insurer or expert opens the public check page without an account, or receives the evidence bundle (.zip) from you, with the photo itself, the manifest, the timestamps and a script to recompute everything, even without Vidimus. The outcome is unchanged or changed.

02 / WITHOUT VIDIMUS

What you can do even without Vidimus.

  • Keep the original file, not the version you forwarded. Messaging services and email programs often shrink photos; then it is a different file.
  • Do not rely on the date in the corner or in the file metadata. It comes from your own device's clock and can be changed with ordinary software.
  • Send the photo to the counterparty or to yourself the same day. An email on an external server is a witness you do not control yourself.
  • If a lot is at stake, ask for a bailiff's report (a finding of the facts by a court bailiff). Vidimus does not replace it.
03 / LIMITS

What it does not prove.

  • That the scene was real. Vidimus records what the camera recorded. Damage that was staged remains staged, but recorded as 'this was reported at the time'.
  • What happened to the photo before it was recorded. A photo that was already on a device only proves that this file existed at the moment of recording. So take the photo in the app, at the location itself.
  • Whether an image was made with AI. Vidimus does not recognise AI images and does not assess what is in a photo.
  • Who was holding the device. The signature belongs to a key on a device of an identified team member; the bundle says that, nothing more.
  • Who is right. Between businesses, evidence is free (Art. 8.11 of the Belgian Civil Code). What the photo shows and how much weight it carries is for the court to assess.

05 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Is a photo with the date and time in the corner not proof?

It is admissible, but weak. That time comes from your own device's clock and is in the image itself; both can be changed. A timestamp from an external service over the fingerprint of the file usually carries more weight, because the time does not come from you.

Why does a forwarded photo give a different fingerprint?

Because it is no longer the same file. Messaging services and email programs often shrink or rewrite photos. So recompute with the photo from the evidence bundle: it has exactly the bytes that were anchored.

Does Vidimus recognise photos made with AI?

No. Vidimus does not assess what is in a photo. It does help with the question that often matters after a suspicion of forgery: did this photo already exist at the moment of the damage, signed and with the location included, or did it only turn up later?

Does the counterparty have to install the app?

No. The counterparty confirms on their own phone, without an account and without an app, and later checks for themselves on the public check page. Your own people use the free app or the web app.

Does this carry weight in court?

Between businesses, evidence is free (Art. 8.11 of the Belgian Civil Code): all means of evidence are admissible and the court weighs them. A photo for which anyone can recompute for themselves when it existed and that it has not changed usually carries more weight than a photo on its own. General information, not legal advice.
