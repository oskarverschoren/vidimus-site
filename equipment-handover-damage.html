VIDIMUS/CASE FILES/EQUIPMENT & MACHINE RENTAL

The dent that "was already there".

Every rental company knows it: the machine leaves in good order, comes back damaged, and the hirer denies it. Your handover report is a PDF from your own system; their word stands against yours. This page shows how to record handover and return so that there is nothing left to deny.

Aerial work platform, handover on Monday morning: in good order, hour meter 1,412, six photos. Return on Friday: boom dented, hydraulic hose damaged. Hirer: "That dent was already there at handover — your photos are of a different machine." Repair: €18,400. Your proof: a PDF that you can make and adjust yourself.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Why this goes wrong so often.

A

The handover report is your document

It comes from your system, on your letterhead, and your people can adjust it. For a court, that is "the rental company's version".

B

The hirer does not always sign, or not knowingly

A scribble on a tablet during a rushed handover convinces few people — certainly if the hirer says they never saw the photos.

C

The return happens without the hirer

Often the machine is dropped off when nobody from your side is there. Then your findings are one-sided — and therefore weak.

02 / TODAY

What you can do now — even without Vidimus.

  • Record the condition together with the hirer: photos of each side, hour meter, serial number, remarks — both signatures, before the machine leaves.
  • Send the handover report with the photos to the hirer straight away; a receipt that is not disputed carries weight.
  • State in your general terms and conditions that the hirer is present at the return or disputes the findings within 24 hours — and add an evidence clause on which records count as proof between the parties, without prejudice to the right to prove the contrary (Art. 8.2 of the Belgian Civil Code).
  • At the return, record again with the same discipline: the same sides, the same order.
03 / THE LAW

What the law says.

Rental law (Art. 1731 of the old Belgian Civil Code) & your general terms and conditions

Without a handover report drawn up in the presence of both parties, the hirer is presumed to have received the goods in the condition in which they return them. The burden of proving that the damage was caused by the hirer lies with you. Your general terms and conditions can regulate this — if the condition at handover is on record.

Art. 8.11 of the Belgian Civil Code

Between businesses, evidence may be given by any means: sensor data, photos and digital reports are admissible. Their weight depends on how independent they are of the party relying on them.

General information, not legal advice. Vidimus provides evidence; how much weight it carries is for the court to assess — and that weight is exactly what anchoring increases.

04 / ANCHORED

What changes.

  • Handover and return are signed by both parties on a phone; photos, hour meter and remarks are hashed at that moment and anchored within five minutes. For customer organisations, the group they are in also usually receives, within six hours, a qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST, a qualified trust service provider on the EU trusted list; each record is linked to it through its Merkle path.
  • Not even your own staff can adjust the report afterwards — that is what convinces the hirer.
  • A damage dispute becomes a comparison of two records that can be checked to be unchanged and to have existed no later than their anchored time.

05 / QUESTIONS

FAQ.

Can sensors (shock, location, hours) be anchored too?

Yes. Each sensor becomes a measurement source, with a unit and a range: each measurement gets a fingerprint and is anchored in batches. That way, the time of the shock is on record too.

The hirer does not want to sign on the phone.

Then your team member signs, and the hirer receives the report and the verification link by email. Not disputing it within the period in your general terms and conditions then counts against them.

Can we see handover and return side by side?

Yes. The dashboard puts two records with the same reference side by side: which fields changed, and the photos per part (front, hour meter, accessories) — with the caption typed in at the moment itself. Each side remains a separate record with its own fingerprint, signature and timestamp; the comparison changes nothing.

Does our rental software have to be replaced?

No. Your software remains the source; we connect via API or webhook, or you use the signing module separately alongside it.
