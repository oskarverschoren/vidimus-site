01 · YOUR CUSTOMER'S PROBLEM

Their records live in your system. The auditor looks there too — and asks whether they have changed since.

Your system shows what was recorded. It cannot show that the record has stayed untouched since — an ordinary database can be changed afterwards, at your customer's and at their counterparty's, and the history that would show it sits in that same database. As long as that cannot be proven, every delivery, measurement or approval in a dispute comes down to one party's word against the other's.

A

Your word against theirs

The recipient says 23 pallets, your customer says 26. Both exports come from a system that is itself in question. Whoever has the nicest PDF wins.

B

The auditor asks for proof

The food safety agency (FAVV), the insurer, the statutory auditor, the judge: they look in the system, then ask whether it has stayed unchanged since and exactly when it was recorded. An export or a screenshot from that same system says nothing about that.

C

Your product gets the blame

"But it was in the system, wasn't it?" Every dispute your customer loses comes back to the software it happened in — yours.

05 · WHAT YOUR CUSTOMER GETS

Not hashing. Proof.

Never say that Vidimus vouches for the content. It establishes who, what, when, and whether anything was changed afterwards — and lets anyone recompute that for themselves.

WHO

Identity

Company number checked in the KBO, the Belgian company register; mandate signed with the eID; a certificate per device or system in the company's name. Every signature is Ed25519, and the key stays on the device.

WHEN

Qualified time

Not our clock: an external timestamp within five minutes, and GLOBALTRUST, a qualified trust service provider on the EU trusted list, puts a qualified timestamp (eIDAS) on the groups containing records of customer organisations, typically within six hours. The same root then goes into Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps — a second witness, public and recomputable without Vidimus.

WHAT

Unchanged

SHA-256 over the data and the attachments, in one Merkle tree per five-minute group. Change one byte and the fingerprint no longer matches the anchor — even if we tried it ourselves.

WITHOUT VIDIMUS

Verification

A public check page, an evidence bundle with a verification script, a case file for the court. Every step can be repeated with open tools. Originals are encrypted per document, with a key per organisation; every decryption by our server appears in the access log, which your customer can see.

07 · QUESTIONS FROM PARTNERS

What you want to know before you sign.

Can I sell it under my own name?

Yes. Co-branding ("your evidence layer — powered by Vidimus") is allowed in your product and documentation. Only the verification itself stays on the Vidimus domain: a check page with only your logo would undermine the separation between who records and who shows the proof, and that separation is exactly what your customer is buying.

Who invoices the customer?

Reseller and integrator: you. You get one monthly invoice from us, with the discount for your level as a separate line. Referral: we invoice the customer and pay out your share every quarter; the amount is shown in your portal every month.

What if my customer wants to go direct, or to another partner?

Then they can. They take their records, keys and verification links with them; you cooperate with the handover. Running referral shares continue until the end of their 24 months.

Am I a commercial agent?

No. You sell in your own name (reseller, integrator) or introduce customers for a fee that is limited in time (referral). There is no exclusivity and no goodwill or clientele indemnity. That is what the agreement says.

What may I say, and what not?

Do say: who, what, when and whether anything was changed afterwards is established, and can be recomputed without Vidimus. Don't say: that Vidimus vouches for the content, that there is a "Bitcoin guarantee", or that a judge has to accept the proof. The judge weighs it; we make it recomputable. The explainer kit in the portal has the right wording.

Measurement or record: what counts for the price?

Measurements from a measuring device (sensor, logger, weighbridge, GPS) are included without limit in € 15 per device for each month in which it measures, the first device free, via /measurements. You create a measuring device with a name, a unit and a range (POST /sources, or per end customer through the partner API); only its own key may send measurements. Anything with its own content or ID — a delivery, photo, CMR or approval — is a record and counts per item, by tier, via /registrations: including what your system sends, and including what still comes in through the old /ingest. See the pricing.

Who reads my customers' content?

Each organisation has its own key; originals are encrypted per document (AES-256-GCM). Your partner key never reads content. If our server reads content to build a case file or bundle, that appears in the access log, which the customer can see.

What if Vidimus stops?

Evidence bundles are open formats with published verification steps (SHA-256, RFC 3161, Merkle, OpenTimestamps). They can still be recomputed without Vidimus. That is set out in the agreement and in the verification guide (in Dutch).
