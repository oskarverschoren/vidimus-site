What you want to know.

Will a court accept this?

The court weighs all evidence freely. Your record is linked, through hash computations you can recompute yourself (Merkle path, Evidence Record), to the root of its group. If that group contains records of a customer organisation, that root carries a qualified timestamp from GLOBALTRUST. For that timestamp, the presumption that its date and time are accurate applies throughout the EU (eIDAS, Art. 41(2)); the record therefore existed no later than that moment. Whether the presumption also applies to this record is for the court to assess. Vidimus shows what was recorded; who is right is for the court to decide.

Do I have to replace my software?

No. Your own software, from ERP to accounting or database, remains the source. Vidimus records and anchors the records that matter, through the app or an integration.

Can Vidimus read my data?

In a regular logbook, everything is stored encrypted on servers in the EU, with a separate key per organisation. Our server opens your content only when the service needs it, for example to display it, search it or build an evidence bundle, and every opening appears in your access log. Want Vidimus to be technically unable to read anything? Choose a confidential logbook: the phone or your system encrypts everything before sending, and only you hold the key. Vidimus then sees the fingerprint and a short envelope (type, reference, source and time), not the content. There is nothing to install; you keep one recovery file.

What counts as one record?

One recorded moment: a delivery, photo, inspection, document or web page. Ten photos of the same delivery, with the signature, together form one record. Sensor measurements do not count separately.

What if Vidimus stops?

If Vidimus stops voluntarily, we announce it at least ninety days in advance and you can take everything with you during that period; in a bankruptcy, the receiver decides. Your bundles remain verifiable: you check every evidence bundle without Vidimus, with a small script or with the European Commission's software.
