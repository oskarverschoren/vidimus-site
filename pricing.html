Questions about the invoice.

What counts as one record?

One recorded moment: a delivery, photo, inspection, document or signature. Ten photos of the same delivery, with the signature, together form one record. If your system sends in documents, such as payslips, invoices or contracts, each document counts as one record, with the file attached, encrypted or as a fingerprint only. Something someone may later pay for or go to court over, such as an offence, a decision or a clock-in time, is also a record, without an attachment.

What is a measurement, and what is not?

A measurement is a single number, such as a temperature, weight or position, from a sensor or logger you created with a unit and a range. You pay per device, not per measurement. What counts depends on the source: everything a system or phone sends is a record.

A record disguised as a measurement does not count as a measurement. The measurement endpoint only accepts the fixed schema (source, time, one number, unit), within the device's unit and range, at most one measurement per ten seconds per device, in ascending time, no older than 24 hours and only with that device's key. A measurement gets no share link and no confirmation by a counterparty: the proof is the series per device. Anyone who misuses the measurement endpoint anyway is charged for those measurements as records, after a warning.

When do I pay the €19 minimum?

Only in a month in which you use more than the free tier. If you use €8 worth, you pay €19. If you stay within the free 100 records and the free device, you pay nothing.

What happens after the free 100 records?

The free account has no end date. From the 101st record in a month, Vidimus asks you to activate your account; until then, nothing more is recorded that month. Activating only asks for a payment method: your company has already been identified with a director's eID, because that happens before the first logbook. Everything already recorded stays stored and can be checked.

How and when do I pay?

Afterwards, per month, for what you used that month. You see your meter reading every day in the app. All prices are excluding VAT.

Can I stop?

Yes, per month. Your bundles remain verifiable without Vidimus. If Vidimus stops voluntarily, you have ninety days to take everything with you; in a bankruptcy, the bankruptcy trustee decides. An annual contract runs for twelve months: if you stop earlier, you pay the rest of that year.

Do my partners in the chain pay too?

No. A carrier, supplier or contractor who co-signs in your logbooks pays nothing. They do first identify their company themselves: a director signs once with the eID. If they also want their own logbooks, they pay for their own use. What your partner records in your logbook does count towards your usage.

Can it go through my software vendor?

Yes. If your TMS, WMS or ERP builds Vidimus in, your vendor invoices you. Same proof, same check. Are you a vendor yourself? Read how becoming a partner works.

Only sensors, no records?

Then you only pay for devices. Four cold stores cost €45 a month: the first device is free, unlimited measurements, evidence bundles and checks included.

Do I need Vidimus?

Four questions make the difference. Is there a counterparty who can dispute, such as a customer, insurer, inspector or subcontractor? Does one incident cost much more than €19 a month, such as a rejected load, an excess or a fine? Is it recorded before there is a dispute? And is there time between recording and disputing? If all of these apply, Vidimus is the cheapest way out of a recurring loss. Work it out.
